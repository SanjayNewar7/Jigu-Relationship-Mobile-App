## Short description
A little space for two: share moments, plan dates, and grow closer.

## Full description
Jigu is a private place for you and your person to keep everyday connection close.

Share photos of your little moments, keep the ones you love, and bring shared memories to your home screen with widgets. Plan a date together with a personal invitation, a place to meet, and reminders.

Celebrate your days together, check in with your mood, and keep a personal journal. When you choose to share your location, your partner can see your latest shared place while you use the app.

Pair using a six-digit invitation code. You control location sharing and can leave your shared space separately from signing out.

Designed with soft lily colours and a gentle, simple feel.
