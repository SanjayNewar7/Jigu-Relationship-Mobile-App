Jigu Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 14 November 2026 App: Jigu: Relationship App (com.sanjaya.jigu) Developer and child safety contact: Sanjaya Rajbhandari, sanjaynewar007@gmail.com

Jigu is a private relationship app for two people. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, and attempts to use Jigu's photos, captions, invitations, or other features to harm a child. Such content and behavior are not allowed even in a private shared space.

Report a concern

Signed-in users can report a child safety concern directly in Jigu > Settings > Child safety > Report a child safety concern. Describe what happened and where it appeared. Do not upload, copy, or resend abusive images. You can also contact sanjaynewar007@gmail.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant child protection authority.

How we respond

We review reports and take action on prohibited content or behavior that comes to our attention. Actions may include removing content, restricting or closing accounts, preserving information when legally required, and reporting to relevant regional or national authorities as applicable law requires. We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities. We do not claim automated detection of every private upload.

Contact

Sanjaya Rajbhandari is Jigu's designated child safety contact and can discuss CSAM prevention practices and compliance. Email sanjaynewar007@gmail.com.

These standards apply to Jigu and may be updated as the app and applicable requirements change. See our privacy policy and terms of use.
