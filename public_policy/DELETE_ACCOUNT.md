# Delete your Jigu account and data

**App:** Jigu (`com.sanjaya.jigu`)  
**Developer:** Sanjaya Rajbhandari  
**Contact:** [sanjaynewar007@gmail.com](mailto:sanjaynewar007@gmail.com?subject=Jigu%20account%20deletion%20request)

To delete your account inside Jigu, open **Settings > Delete account**. Enter your current password, type **DELETE**, and choose **Permanently delete account**. Your signed-in account is deleted immediately after verification. You can also email **sanjaynewar007@gmail.com** with the subject **Jigu account deletion request** from your account email if you cannot access the app. Do not include your password or invitation code in email.

In-app deletion removes your account, private mood and journal entries, location, notification tokens, and every shared space you belong to. Shared moments, date plans, saved places, and stored photos in those spaces are removed for both people, and your partner is unpaired. Jigu cannot delete a photo that either person saved outside the app to a phone gallery. For an email request, the developer will verify account ownership and process the same deletion.

Logging out and leaving a shared space do not delete an account. To ask for deletion of a specific shared photo or other content, describe it in your request.

See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for information about data collection and retention.
